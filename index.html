Our principles

Our Approach to Government

We believe in creating opportunities for people to succeed, spending public money responsibly, protecting individual freedoms, and delivering the essential services British Columbians depend on.

Our focus is on practical solutions that work for BC, not political ideology or division.

Questions that deserve clear answers

Where We Stand

These are questions British Columbians may have about the party. Answers are intentionally left for the party to draft and confirm.

Illustrative draft responses for discussion only — the party should review, confirm, and revise each answer to reflect its actual commitments.

Will you privatize health care?

No. We believe everyone should have access to publicly funded health care, regardless of their ability to pay.

Does smaller government mean cutting essential services?

No. Our goal is to spend public money responsibly while protecting the essential services British Columbians depend on.

Do you believe in climate change?

Yes. Climate change is real. We believe BC needs practical solutions that protect communities and the environment.

Are you aligned with Donald Trump?

No. We are focused on British Columbia, not importing the political divisions or agenda of the United States.

Will you restrict abortion access?

No. We support a woman’s right to choose and believe reproductive health decisions belong to individuals, not politicians.

Do you support LGBTQ+ rights?

Yes. We support equal rights, dignity, and protection from discrimination for LGBTQ+ British Columbians.
